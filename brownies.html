Fudgy Chocolate Brownies

Extremely rich, intensely chocolatey, and wonderfully fudgy with a delicate crinkly top. A chocolate lover's dream!

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, sliced into 12 Tbsp-size pieces
  • 4 ounces semi-sweet chocolate, coarsely chopped and divided
  • 1 and 2/3 cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 1 cup unsweetened natural or Dutch-process cocoa powder
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour (spooned & leveled)
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and line a 9-inch square metal baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. In a small microwave-safe bowl or liquid measuring cup, combine the butter and half of the chopped chocolate. Melt in 30-second increments, stirring after each, until smooth and completely melted. Set aside to slightly cool.
  3. In a large bowl with a handheld or stand mixer fitted with a whisk attachment, combine the sugar and eggs. Beat on medium-high speed until the sugar is dissolved and the mixture is pale and ribbon-y (about 3 minutes).
  4. Pour the warm melted butter and chocolate mixture into the bowl with the sugar and eggs, add the vanilla extract, and beat or whisk to combine.
  5. Using a fine mesh sieve, sift the cocoa powder and flour into the batter, add the salt, then whisk or beat on low speed to incorporate.
  6. Fold in the remaining chopped chocolate and the chocolate chips. The batter will be very thick. Spread it evenly into the prepared pan using a small offset spatula.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes. Test with a toothpick inserted into the center; if there are only a few moist crumbs, the brownies are done.
  8. Remove from the oven and place on a cooling rack to cool completely in the pan. When cool, lift out using the parchment overhangs, transfer to a cutting board, and cut into squares with a sharp knife.

Baker's Tip

Using a metal baking pan rather than glass helps the edges bake evenly while keeping the center luxuriously fudgy and moist!
