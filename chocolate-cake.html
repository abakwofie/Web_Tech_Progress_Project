Ultimate Chocolate Cake

A deeply rich, moist, and decadent chocolate cake topped with velvety chocolate fudge frosting. A true crowd-pleaser!

Ingredients

  • 1 and 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 and 3/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons espresso powder (optional)
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup buttermilk, room temperature
  • 1 cup hot coffee or water

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (177°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and espresso powder together in a large bowl
  3. In a separate bowl, mix the oil, eggs, and vanilla together on medium-high speed until combined
  4. Add the buttermilk and mix until combined
  5. Pour the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients, add the hot coffee/water, and mix until the batter is completely combined
  6. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans
  7. Bake for 25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean

Baking Note

I would advise you to use the espresso power for added flavours!
