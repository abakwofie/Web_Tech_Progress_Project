Southern Red Velvet Cake

A stunning crimson-colored cake with a mild cocoa flavor and rich tang, folded with fluffy whipped egg whites for a silky crumb. A timeless favorite!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cake flour (spooned & leveled)
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 Tablespoons unsweetened natural cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 cup canola or vegetable oil
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature and separated
  • 1 Tablespoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon distilled white vinegar
  • Liquid or gel red food coloring
  • 1 cup buttermilk, at room temperature

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Grease two 9-inch cake pans.
  2. Make the cake: Whisk the flour, baking soda, cocoa powder, and salt together in a large bowl. Set aside.
  3. Using a handheld or stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, beat the butter and sugar together on medium-high speed until combined, about 1 minute. Scrape down the sides and bottom of the bowl as needed.
  4. Add the oil, egg yolks, vanilla extract, and vinegar, and beat on high for 2 minutes (set the egg whites aside). Scrape down the bowl as needed.
  5. With the mixer on low speed, add the dry ingredients in 2-3 additions alternating with the buttermilk. Beat in your desired amount of food coloring (typically 1-2 teaspoons of gel food coloring) just until combined.
  6. In a separate medium bowl, vigorously whisk or beat the 4 egg whites on high speed until fluffy peaks form (about 3 minutes). Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the cake batter until silky and slightly thick.
  7. Divide the batter evenly between the cake pans. Bake for 30 minutes or until the tops of the cakes spring back when gently touched and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

Baker's Tip

When folding in the whipped egg whites, use a gentle spatula motion to avoid deflating the air you just whipped into them—this is the secret to a light texture!
