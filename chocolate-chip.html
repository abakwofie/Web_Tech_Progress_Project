Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies

Loaded with semi-sweet chocolate chips and packed with rich flavor from brown sugar and honey. An absolute classic!

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (spooned & leveled)
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 10 Tablespoons unsalted butter, melted + slightly cooled
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup packed light or dark brown sugar
  • 2 Tablespoons honey or light corn syrup
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 2 Tablespoons milk
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 1 and 1/4 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. If not chilling the cookie dough, preheat oven to 375°F (191°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl. Set aside.
  3. Whisk the melted butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, honey, egg yolk, milk, and vanilla extract together until combined. Pour into dry ingredients and mix completely, then fold in the chocolate chips. The dough will be soft.
  4. Choose your preferred thickness: do not chill for thin and crisp cookies, chill for 1 hour for slightly thicker cookies, or chill for 2 hours for thick cookies with soft centers and crisp edges. (If chilling, remember to preheat the oven afterwards!)
  5. Roll cookie dough into balls (1 scant Tablespoon per cookie) and arrange 2 inches apart on the baking sheets.
  6. Bake for 10 minutes or until browned on top and around the sides.
  7. Remove from the oven and allow cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 3 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

Baker's Tip

Adding a touch of honey or corn syrup alongside the brown sugar helps retain moisture, giving these cookies their signature chewy texture!
