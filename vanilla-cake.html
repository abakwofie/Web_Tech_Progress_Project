Classic Vanilla Bean Cake

A tall, fluffy, and incredibly moist scratch-made vanilla cake loaded with real vanilla flavor. A true bakery classic!

Ingredients

  • 3 and 2/3 cups cake flour (spooned & leveled)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 and 1/2 cups unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs + 2 additional egg whites, at room temperature
  • 1 Tablespoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 and 1/2 cups buttermilk, at room temperature

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Grease three 9-inch cake pans
  2. Whisk the cake flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda together in a bowl and set aside.
  3. Using a handheld or stand mixer fitted with a paddle or whisk attachment, beat the butter and sugar together on high speed until smooth and creamy (about 3 minutes), scraping down the bowl as needed.
  4. Beat in the 3 eggs, 2 egg whites, and vanilla extract on high speed until combined (about 2 minutes). The mixture may look slightly curdled.
  5. With the mixer on low speed, add the dry ingredients just until combined, then pour in the buttermilk and mix until incorporated, whisking gently by hand if needed to remove lumps.
  6. Pour batter evenly into the prepared cake pans and bake for 25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean

Baker's Tip

Always make sure your buttermilk and eggs are genuinely at room temperature so the batter emulsifies smoothly!
